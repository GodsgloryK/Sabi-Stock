About us

About Us

Smart Stock was built to stop business owners from losing money to poor stock management.Whether you run a small shop, a restaurant, or an online store, Smart Stock helps you track products, inventory, orders, sales, and performance in one lightweight dashboard.

Features

All features

Everything you need to run your store, in one place.

Track stock in real time

Know exactly what you have, with low-stock and out-of-stock badges and a configurable threshold.

Record sales fast

Pick from live stock, auto-decrement inventory, and snapshot prices and profit on each sale.

Work as a team

Invite stock managers with single-use 24-hour codes, revoke invitations, and manage access easily.

AI forecasting & insights

Get a restock briefing, reorder-by dates, suggested quantities, and anomalies for spikes, drops, stale stock, and below-cost sales.

Grounded AI assistant

Answers based on your live business snapshot — today's sales/profit, low stock, top sellers, and urgent reorders.

Reports & export

Export products and sales as CSV with date and product filters for quick record keeping.

Guide

How to utilise all features

A quick guide to get the most out of Smart Stock.

Dashboard

See KPIs, best sellers, 30-day trends, and low-stock at a glance. Auto-refreshes every 60 seconds.

Products

Add or edit products with a unique Product ID, search by name or ID, filter by category, and watch low-stock badges.

All stock

Quickly review all products and quantities to spot what needs restocking.

Sales

Record sales from live stock, filter by date or product, view paginated history, and void & restock when needed.

Insights (AI)

Check the AI briefing, ranked restock list, demand chart (history + forecast), and "needs attention" anomalies.

Team & Settings (Owner)

Invite or revoke stock managers, remove members, and set your low-stock threshold and timezone.
